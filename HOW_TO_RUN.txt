BRAND FINDER - HOW TO USE
=========================

STEP 1 - Open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type "terminal", press Enter)

STEP 2 - Go to the brand-finder folder:
cd ~/Desktop/brand-finder

STEP 3 - Run with brands you type directly:
python3 brand_finder.py --no-claude "Nike" "Sony" "Zara"

        OR run with your brands.txt file:
python3 brand_finder.py --no-claude --file brands.txt

STEP 4 - Open results in Excel/Numbers:
open ~/Desktop/brand-finder/results.csv


TO ADD/CHANGE BRANDS IN brands.txt:
open ~/Desktop/brand-finder/brands.txt
